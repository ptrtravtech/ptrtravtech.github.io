1. Introduction

Welcome to Home Compass (宜居罗盘), developed by PtrtravTech. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By downloading or using Home Compass, you agree to the terms described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Key Summary: Home Compass is a local-first app. Floor plans, exterior photos, home records, and compass readings are stored only on your device and are analysed on the device. We do not request location permission. We use third-party services (Firebase and Google AdMob) that may collect limited usage data as described below.

2. Information We Collect

Local Only Data You Create – Stored On-Device

The following data is created and stored exclusively on your device. PtrtravTech does not have access to this data:

  • Floor plan images and markers: photos or files you choose, and the rooms, doors, and centre you mark, used to create floor-plan Feng Shui reports
  • Exterior photos: photos of windows, balconies, and the building surroundings, used for on-device exterior analysis
  • Home records: names, balcony facing, reports, and comparison notes you keep for each home
  • Compass readings: heading used to measure balcony facing; readings never leave your device
  • App preferences: language, theme, and related settings

The App does not collect your date of birth and does not request location permission.

On-device Floor-Plan Text Recognition – Powered by Google ML Kit

When you photograph a floor plan, Home Compass uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition to read the room names and labels printed on it, so it can place markers for you.

  • The recognition model is bundled inside the app and runs entirely on your device.
  • Your image is never uploaded to Google or any other server in order to be recognised.
  • No device identifier, account, or location is sent while recognising.
  • If you never photograph or import a floor plan, this feature is never used.

ML Kit is provided by Google LLC. See the ML Kit Terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics Usage Data – Collected via Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app. Firebase Analytics may automatically collect:

  • App session duration and frequency
  • Screen views and navigation patterns
  • Device type, OS version, and language setting
  • App version and first open events
  • General geographic region (country-level, not precise location)

This data is aggregated and pseudonymized. It is used solely to improve app performance and user experience. Firebase Analytics data is processed by Google LLC. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Crash Reports Crash Data – Collected via Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically detect and diagnose app crashes. Crashlytics may collect:

  • Stack traces and error logs at the time of a crash
  • Device model, OS version, and app version
  • A randomly generated Crashlytics installation ID (not linked to your identity)

This data is used only to fix bugs and improve stability. No personally identifiable information is included in crash reports. Crash collection is not enabled in debug builds.

Advertising Advertising Data – Collected via Google AdMob

Home Compass displays ads powered by Google AdMob, including banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android Ad ID) to deliver personalized ads
  • Interaction data (ad views, clicks)
  • Device and network information for ad targeting
  • IP address (used to determine approximate location for ad relevance)

You can opt out of personalized advertising by resetting or opting out of your device's Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (Android). See Google's Ads Settings for more options.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

On-device data (floor plans, photos, reports, home records, settings) is retained until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Deleting a home deletes its reports as well. We do not have the ability to delete data stored on your device.

Analytics and crash data collected by Firebase is retained according to Google's standard data retention policies (typically up to 14 months for Analytics). You may request deletion by contacting us at ptrtravtech@gmail.com.

Advertising data is managed by Google AdMob according to Google's data retention policies.

7. How to request deletion of your data

For Google Play & data safety disclosures: You may request deletion of personal data associated with your use of Home Compass that is processed by us or by our service providers (including Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google AdMob).

To submit a data deletion request:

  1. Email us at ptrtravtech@gmail.com.
  2. Use a subject line such as: Home Compass – Data deletion request.
  3. Briefly describe what you want deleted (e.g. analytics/crash data associated with your use of the app). Including your country/region helps us apply the correct legal requirements.

We will acknowledge your request and respond within 30 days where required by applicable law, or sooner when practicable. We may need to verify your request to protect your privacy.

Data stored only on your device (floor plans, photos, reports, home records, app settings) is not on our servers. To remove it, delete the content in the app or uninstall Home Compass. Uninstalling the app typically erases locally stored app data on your device.

Google-held data: You can also manage data tied to your Google account and ad personalization at Google Data & privacy and Google Ads Settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Home Compass is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at ptrtravtech@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a summary of what data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of any personal data we hold — see Section 7 for step-by-step instructions (note: on-device data must be deleted by you or by uninstalling the app).
  • Opt-out of personalized ads: Go to your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset or opt out of your Advertising ID.
  • Opt-out of analytics: Contact us at ptrtravtech@gmail.com to request that Firebase Analytics data collection be disabled for your device.

Residents of the European Economic Area (EEA) have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to object to processing and the right to data portability. Residents of California (USA) have rights under the CCPA, including the right to know and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at ptrtravtech@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Data Security

All data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's built-in security (e.g., device encryption, app sandboxing). Floor-plan and exterior recognition run on the device. Data transmitted by third-party services (Firebase, AdMob) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to protect your device with a screen lock.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. When we do, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify you through an in-app notice or an app update.

Your continued use of Home Compass after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

仅本地 您创建的数据 — 仅存储在您的设备上

端侧处理 户型图文字识别 — 由 Google ML Kit 提供

拍摄户型图时，宜居罗盘使用 Google ML Kit 文字识别 读取图上印制的房间名称和标注，以便自动为房间打点。

  • 识别模型随应用一起打包，完全在您的设备上运行。
  • 图片不会为了识别而上传到 Google 或任何其他服务器。
  • 识别过程中不发送设备标识符、账号或位置信息。
  • 如果您从不拍摄或导入户型图，该能力不会被使用。

ML Kit 由 Google LLC 提供。详见 ML Kit 服务条款与 Google 隐私政策。

分析 使用数据 — 通过 Firebase Analytics 收集

我们使用 Google Firebase Analytics 了解用户如何使用本应用。Firebase Analytics 可能自动收集：

  • 应用使用时长及频率

  • 设备类型、操作系统版本及语言设置
  • 应用版本及首次启动事件

上述数据经过汇总和假名化处理，仅用于改善应用性能和用户体验。Firebase Analytics 数据由 Google LLC 处理。详见 Google 隐私政策。

我们使用 Firebase Crashlytics 自动检测和诊断应用崩溃。Crashlytics 可能收集：

  • 崩溃时的堆栈跟踪和错误日志

这些数据仅用于修复错误和提升稳定性，崩溃报告中不含任何可识别个人身份的信息。调试版本不会开启崩溃收集。

您可以通过前往设备的「设置 → 隐私 → 广告」重置或退出广告 ID，从而选择退出个性化广告。更多选项请参见 Google 广告设置。
