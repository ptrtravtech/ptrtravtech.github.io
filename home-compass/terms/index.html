2. What the App Does

Home Compass is an analysis aid for living spaces. It provides:

  • Floor-plan analysis: recognises rooms, doors, and windows on a floor plan and describes orientation, daylight, ventilation, and circulation
  • Exterior analysis: organises information about the surroundings from photos of windows or balconies you take
  • Compass: measures facing using your device's orientation sensor
  • Home records: saves and compares analyses for multiple homes

Analysis runs on your device. No account and no phone number are required.
